# پروین بانو کا گوشۂ سکون | Parveen Banu's Sanctuary of Peace

A dedicated, mobile-first peaceful sanctuary and digital love letter crafted with love for Ammi Jaan (Parveen Banu).

---

## 🚀 How to Publish Online

### Option A: Netlify Drop (Instant, Zero Setup - 15 Seconds)
1. Open [https://app.netlify.com/drop](https://app.netlify.com/drop) in your browser.
2. Drag and drop this entire `mumma app` folder into the Netlify box.
3. Your website will be live immediately with a free permanent link (e.g., `https://parveen-banu.netlify.app`) that you can send to your mother.

---

### Option B: GitHub Pages
1. Create a new repository on [GitHub](https://github.com/new) named `mumma-app` (or any name you like).
2. Open your terminal and run:
   ```bash
   cd "/home/omarnazeer/mumma app"
   git remote add origin https://github.com/<YOUR_GITHUB_USERNAME>/<REPO_NAME>.git
   git push -u origin main
   ```
3. On GitHub, go to your repository **Settings** > **Pages**.
4. Under **Branch**, select `main` (folder: `/ (root)`) and click **Save**.
5. Your website will be live in 1 minute at:
   `https://<YOUR_GITHUB_USERNAME>.github.io/<REPO_NAME>/`

## ✨ Features Included
- **Bilingual Experience:** Graceful Urdu Nastaliq calligraphy alongside gentle English translations for every single section.
- **Time-Aware Greeting:** Automatically greets Ammi based on morning, afternoon, evening, or night.
- **Ammi's Mood Check-in:** Personalized comforting replies and prayers based on how she feels.
- **Ammi's Tea Nook:** Steaming porcelain tea cup with rotating sweet reflections on her tea.
- **The Unfolding Letter of Apology & Love:** Royal wax-sealed letter in Urdu and English honoring her and seeking forgiveness.
- **10 Reasons of Love:** Interactive blooming bouquet of jasmine and roses.
- **Tactile Pearl Tasbih:** Emerald prayer bead with realistic click sound, haptics, and daily dhikr presets.
- **Daily Dua Jar:** Personalized Quranic and Sunnah duas for her health, peace of mind, and Jannah.
- **Sincere Intentions & Efforts:** Filial devotion checklist in Urdu and English.
- **Halal Soothing Rain:** Procedural Web Audio synthesizer (100% halal, no music).
- **Mobile PWA Ready:** Add to Home Screen on iPhone or Android to open like an app.
